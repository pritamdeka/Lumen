Medical disclaimer

Spasht is an informational tool that translates and explains text found in medical documents. It is not a medical device, does not provide a diagnosis, and does not recommend treatment or medication changes.

Check the original

Automated extraction and explanations can be incorrect or incomplete. The original medical document remains authoritative. Confirm every value, unit, reference range, medicine name, and instruction with the original document and a qualified healthcare professional.

Reference ranges

Spasht uses only ranges printed on the uploaded report. A result inside or outside a printed range does not by itself establish whether you are healthy or have a condition.

Urgent concerns

Spasht cannot reliably detect every urgent situation. If you have severe symptoms, feel unsafe, or believe someone needs urgent care, contact the appropriate local emergency service or a qualified clinician immediately.

Medication

Never start, stop, or change medication because of a Spasht explanation. Follow instructions from an appropriately qualified professional.
