# Product and Revenue Roadmap

## Trust foundation (current)

- Complete locale coverage, native-speaker review, RTL support, multi-page analysis, and automated regression tests.
- Keep uploaded images and report history out of cloud storage. Store history only in the browser.
- Add extraction-confidence warnings and user confirmation before interpreting uncertain values.

## Useful local features

- Extract PDF pages in the browser before upload.
- Add accessible voice playback and local-only report trends.
- Add privacy, terms, contact, consent, data export, and deletion guidance.
- Measure only anonymous funnel events; never send report values, filenames, images, or user-entered medical text to analytics.

## Consumer freemium

After reliability and legal review, add accounts that store only identity, usage entitlement, and payment references. Start with two free analyses per month and test a ₹49 five-report pack through Razorpay. If repeat usage supports it, test ₹99/month for 15 reports plus voice, trends, and family profiles. Urgent safety guidance must never be hidden behind payment.

## Domain launch checklist

- Connect apex and `www` to Vercel, force HTTPS, and create a domain-based support email.
- Publish privacy, terms, refund, contact, and medical-boundary pages before accepting payments.
- Create non-personal educational landing pages for search discovery. Do not place ads in report or history views.
- Obtain professional Indian privacy, consumer, and medical-device legal review before monetization.

## Later opportunities

Validate clinic, laboratory, pharmacy, and NGO demand only after consumer reliability is established. A B2B edition should have separate access controls, contracts, audit requirements, and data-retention policy; it must not reuse consumer local history implicitly.
